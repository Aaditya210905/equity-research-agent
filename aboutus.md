# About EquityOS

## AI-Powered Equity Research, Built for Smarter Analysis

**EquityOS** is an AI-powered equity research platform designed to help users explore companies, understand financial performance, and analyze publicly available financial information through an automated research workflow.

Instead of simply generating answers from an AI model, EquityOS combines **financial data, document-based research, semantic retrieval, deterministic financial calculations, and AI-driven analysis** to produce structured and evidence-grounded research reports.

## How EquityOS Works

EquityOS follows an autonomous research workflow:

**Data Collection → Document Processing → RAG Retrieval → Financial Analysis → AI Research → Verification → Research Report**

The platform can analyze information such as:

* Company financial statements
* Annual reports and investor documents
* Financial ratios and growth metrics
* Market data
* Relevant company news
* Risks and business developments
* Competitive and historical trends

AI-generated insights are grounded in retrieved evidence, while financial calculations are performed using deterministic computation rather than relying on the language model for arithmetic.

## Our Goal

Our goal is to make equity research **more accessible, transparent, and efficient** by combining traditional financial analysis with modern AI technologies.

EquityOS is designed to help users:

* Understand complex financial information
* Explore company fundamentals
* Discover relevant information from lengthy reports
* Compare companies and financial metrics
* Identify important risks and opportunities
* Trace AI-generated insights back to their sources

## Built with Responsible AI

EquityOS is designed with an emphasis on **accuracy, transparency, and verification**.

The platform uses source citations and automated verification mechanisms to reduce unsupported AI-generated claims. However, AI systems and financial data sources can contain errors, omissions, delays, or inaccuracies.

Users should independently verify important information before relying on it.

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

EquityOS provides AI-generated financial research for **educational and informational purposes only**. It does not constitute investment, financial, or personalized advice, nor does it constitute a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security.

EquityOS does not assess an individual's financial circumstances, investment objectives, or risk tolerance. Users are responsible for their own investment decisions and should consult a qualified financial professional when personalized advice is required.
